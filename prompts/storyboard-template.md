## Notes column suggestions

- "Use BUSY logo SVG" / "use product logo here"
- "Field reveals — 5 fields, stagger 10f each"
- "Speed-blur on word X frames Y-Z"
- "Hold +0.3s before exit for readability"
- "Phone-app UI for mobile, browser for landscape"

## Music sync check

Before signing off, verify scene boundaries fall **close to** musical bars (within 4-6 frames). At 120 BPM: bars at 60, 120, 180, 240, etc. Most scenes 2-4 bars long.
